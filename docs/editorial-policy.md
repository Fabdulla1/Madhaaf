# Madhaaf editorial and evidence standards

## Scope and identity

Madhaaf documents Mombasa and Ethiopian Kutchi through linguistic and cultural research. Use "Mombasa and Ethiopian Kutchi" or "East African Kutchi" when referring to the project's scope. Identify the variety represented by particular speaker examples where known. Do not suggest that a Mombasa example has been independently attested in Ethiopia.

Describe Madhaaf as a research and documentation project, not a personal hobby, passion project, or individual ownership of a dialect. Credit Farhan Abdulla as founder and researcher, along with documented collaborators and supporters. Do not imply institutional endorsement, independent peer review or broader speaker validation without evidence.

## Research writing

Write plainly and precisely. Prefer "The recorded examples show..." to autobiographical framing. Use short sentences and meaningful headings. Avoid marketing slogans, inflated claims, formulaic language and decorative em dashes in articles. Avoid emoji in mobile-facing interface labels; text navigation must remain usable without symbols.

Keep clear distinctions between:

- A speaker-supplied sentence and translation.
- A speaker's explicit judgment about meaning or grammaticality.
- An analyst's proposed grammatical explanation.
- A comparison with published Gujarati, Sindhi or other Kutchi varieties.
- An open question that still requires further elicitation.

Do not silently correct Kutchi spellings or translations, guess morphological endings, or fill unanswered questions as though they were speaker evidence. Preserve original forms even when they do not follow the provisional orthography.

## Sources and publication

Record sources and consent as required for oral histories, media and family records. Separate oral tradition from independently verified historical claims. State limitations when evidence comes from only one speaker or community. Article issue dates are retrospective editorial dates in 2026; first online publication was in October 2026. Do not backdate publication metadata.

## Editorial review checklist

Before publishing a change, review homepage and About text, the language and writing guides, research article titles and introductions, metadata, navigation, mobile views, and tests. Check for old personal framing, invisible-to-the-reader uncertainty, editorial em dashes, and mobile emoji or arrow decorations. Preserve external links by redirecting any renamed article routes.
